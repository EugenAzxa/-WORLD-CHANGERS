02 — New

Opening film New

The home page now opens with a 12-second film. The camera starts above the clouds with white doves and descends over Rome to St. Peter’s Square. It ends on a terrace facing the dome, where Sir Prof. Dr. Gabriele Pao-Pei Andreoli holds the IASC medal and says: “Hello, and welcome to the World Changers Summit.”

How it behaves

  • Opening sequence. The film fills the screen with a Skip intro button and a progress line. When it ends it holds on the welcome for a moment, then scrolls down to the website by itself.
  • Sound. The film tries to play with sound. Most browsers block sound until the visitor interacts, so in that case it starts silently and the first click or tap turns the voice on. If the greeting has already passed, the film rewinds to it. A highlighted “Tap for sound” button is shown meanwhile.
  • Captions. Place captions and an English subtitle of the greeting are shown, so the film works without sound too.
  • Repeat visits. It plays once per visit. When visitors come back to the home page, they see the welcome frame with a play button.
  • Reduced motion. Visitors who turn off animations in their device settings never get autoplay.
Please note: the film was generated with AI (Higgsfield), from public photographs of the founder and a photograph of the IASC medal. The voice is synthetic, not Prof. Andreoli’s own. It is on the public preview link now. We need Prof. Andreoli’s approval to keep it there and before it appears on wcsiasc.org. We can replace the voice with a real recording at any time.

03 — New

Interactive globe with a real Earth New

The centre of the site is a globe of every World Changers gathering. It now shows a photographic Earth made from NASA Blue Marble imagery, with drifting clouds, a thin atmosphere and stars. Host cities are marked on it. Lines from the Vatican trace how the movement travelled.

  • Explore. Drag to turn, zoom in and out, and filter by year (2023–2027).
  • Select a city. This opens what happened there: events, dates, venues and links.
  • Play the journey. The globe flies from gathering to gathering in chronological order.
  • Share a place. Every place has its own link, for example …/world-map/#place=dubai.
  • The 2023 journey. An animated map of the whole story, available from the home page.

05 — New

Catalogue 2025 on the website New

The 78-page “Presentation 2025” catalogue used to open in a WordPress flip-book plugin. It is now a page of the website itself (/catalogue/):

  • Reading. Two-page spreads with a page-turn effect on computers, single pages on phones. Turn pages with the arrow keys, a swipe or a click. There is also full-screen mode and a grid of all 78 pages.
  • Contents. A table of contents for each edition (1st to 8th), the awards and the new events. Each entry links to the matching event page on the website.
  • Text version. The full text of every chapter is included, for search engines, translation and screen readers.
  • Page links. Any page can be linked directly, for example …/catalogue/#p=45 (Davos 2025).

07 — New

Accessibility: “Listen to this page” New

For visitors with low vision, or anyone who prefers to listen, every page with text now has a Listen button in the bottom-left corner. It shows the reading time, for example “Listen · 3 min”.

  • What it reads. The page title, the introduction and the main text.
  • Following along. The paragraph being read is highlighted and the page scrolls with it.
  • Controls. Pause, resume, stop, and three speeds (1×, 1.25× and 0.85×). Double-clicking a paragraph while listening starts reading from there.
  • How it works. It uses the speech voice built into the visitor’s own phone or computer, so it costs nothing and needs no outside service. Voice quality depends on the device; Apple devices sound best.

Other accessibility work

  • Contrast. Text contrast meets WCAG AA. Buttons are at least 44 × 44 px on touch screens.
  • Keyboard and screen readers. Menus, search, the catalogue and the film controls work with the keyboard. Images have descriptions, and the globe has a plain list of places for keyboard and screen-reader users.
  • Motion. Animations respect the “reduce motion” setting of the device.
  • Film. The film has on-screen captions and a subtitle of the spoken greeting.

08 — Checked

Mobile version

All 415 pages were opened at phone width (390 px) and checked automatically for sideways scrolling, content running off the screen, text squeezed into narrow columns and text touching the screen edge. Problems found and fixed:

  • Articles. Text touched the edges of the screen; side margins are restored.
  • Long web links. Links inside articles pushed the page wider; they now wrap.
  • Gallery title. The “Ukraine Reconstruction Summit” title broke in the middle of a word. A hidden non-breaking space in the old title caused it.
  • Lists. List items with a heading were squeezed into a narrow column.
  • Film. On phones the film now fills the whole screen.

After the fixes no page scrolls sideways on a phone.

12 — For the technical team

Technical notes

  • Platform. A fast static website (Astro), hosted on Vercel and published automatically from GitHub (EugenAzxa/-WORLD-CHANGERS). No database or plugins to maintain.
  • Re-importing content. Scripts re-import content from the current WordPress site, re-check every page against it and check all links. The Catalogue and the film each have a script to rebuild them.
  • Search engines. Sitemap, canonical addresses and social-media previews are generated for every page.
  • Licences. Earth imagery is from NASA Blue Marble (public domain, credited on the globe). The film was generated with Higgsfield (Seedance 2.5 and Seedream 5.0).
